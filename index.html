How Dropout Risk Score is Calculated (Rule-Based Model)

EduGuard uses a transparent weighted scoring algorithm (0% to 100%) based on 4 key academic & behavioral indicators tracked by colleges:

1. Attendance Weight (Max 40 pts) (85 - Attendance%) × 0.8
2. CGPA Weight (Max 30 pts) (8.0 - CGPA) × 6.5
3. Assignments Weight (Max 15 pts) (85 - Assignments%) × 0.35
4. Backlogs / Fee Hold (Max 25 pts) +15 pts (or +25 if both)
